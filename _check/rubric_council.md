# Tally rubric v1 (frozen 2026-11-07) - council format, same criteria, anchors and weights
Hard gates are tool-checked by code and are NOT judged here (all currently pass).

- S1 Visual polish, hierarchy, consistency in light and dark (w=0.25): 4 = uneven spacing, generic, 7 = clean with a few rough edges, 10 = every screen reads like a shipped finance app
- S2 Capture flow: speak to saved, correction, undo (w=0.25): 4 = several taps or unclear state, 7 = works with minor friction, 10 = one tap, live feedback, instant fix and undo
- S3 Summaries answer "how am I doing?" (w=0.20): 4 = totals only, 7 = totals plus trend plus categories, 10 = plus budget pace, comparisons and drill-down
- S4 Share card quality (w=0.15): 4 = cluttered or breaks, 7 = good with minor layout issues, 10 = poster-grade in every mode, theme and language
- S5 Robustness: empty and error states, offline, data safety (w=0.15): 4 = breaks on edge cases, 7 = handles common cases, 10 = graceful everywhere, data never lost
