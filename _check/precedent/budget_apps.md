These apps use distinct UI patterns to communicate budget health and category activity on mobile.

Monarch Money and Copilot Money prioritize progress bars and color-coded status indicators [1][2]. Both offer granular category management screens where spending is visualized against defined budgets; Copilot specifically uses animated budget dials and progress bars to show real-time status [2]. Monarch provides flexible category views and allows for monthly and yearly spending forecasts [1].

Toshl and Money Lover utilize more complex chart-based visualizations [3][4][5]. Toshl uses a progress bar with a blue-to-red lollipop indicator, where the lollipop moves as spending increases, and a grey upside-down lollipop marks the current day to show budget pace [4][5]. Toshl displays categories without budgets in a separate Remaining Budgets section [4][5]. Money Lover uses column charts where colored bars represent spending relative to a budget line; it explicitly calculates projected monthly spending based on the number of days elapsed and total transactions [3].

Across these platforms, empty states are handled by presenting clear calls-to-action—such as prompts to connect accounts, add transactions, or assign funds—rather than showing empty charts or zeroed values [2][6].
