Several expense trackers allow natural-language voice input. They universally prioritize a review-before-save pattern, where voice input generates an editable draft.

Nami [1], Fineyo [2], Kesef [3], Folko [4], Granite [5], Receiptix [6], Carlo [7], and MoneyTalks [8] all support this.

Interaction patterns for these apps follow a consistent flow:
1. Trigger: Tap a microphone icon within the app to start dictation [2][4][6][7][8].
2. Input: Speak the transaction naturally (e.g., "Lunch 12 and taxi 8"). Some apps like Nami, Kesef, and Receiptix explicitly support parsing multiple expenses in one sentence [1][3][6], while others like Granite prefer individual entries [5].
3. Review: The app displays a draft with parsed fields (amount, merchant, category). You can tap any field to correct details [2][3][4][5][7][8].
4. Confirm/Undo: No data is saved to your ledger until you manually confirm [1][3][4][5][8]. To undo or cancel, you can use a cancel button on the review screen or, in the case of Nami, use a one-tap undo feature after saving [1][7].
